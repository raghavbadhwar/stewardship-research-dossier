# Live research reliability and depth pilot

This runbook is for an explicitly authorized fresh public-issuer pilot. It is not evidence of
host loading, live retrieval, or analyst approval until the corresponding actions are performed.

1. Open a new run (never reuse `nvidia-governance-2026:0001`) and save a 1–12 issue plan.
2. Collect evidence in at least two batches, preserve every per-URL failure, then call
   `collection_finish` once.
3. Where an approved local official PDF is used, retain its original public URL, publisher,
   title, and capture time. Do not bypass a denial, robot policy, or rate limit.
4. Read relevant source sections in full, update coverage and issues, save an unapproved draft,
   and inspect draft diagnostics. Do not approve the packet.
5. Record outcomes, including 403, 429, redirects, MIME rejection, or size rejection, and stop.

Required evidence: structured MCP collection result; final run state; saved plan and draft;
diagnostics; source ledger hashes; and reviewer notes. Host refresh and live network work require
separate authorization.
